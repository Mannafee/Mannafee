### 🧠 What I do

I build the parts of an AI product that have to work when it matters:

- **Generative AI** — LLM applications, RAG systems, evaluation harnesses, and prompt/context engineering that hold up outside the demo.
- **Agentic AI** — multi-step, tool-using agents with the guardrails, observability, and fallbacks that make autonomy safe to deploy.
- **MLOps** — the pipelines, CI/CD, monitoring, and infrastructure that take a model from a notebook to a dependable service.

I work end to end: framing the problem, choosing the right architecture, and owning it through to production and iteration. My background spans applied machine learning research (EEG-based emotion analysis, distributed ML) and modern cloud-native AI engineering.
